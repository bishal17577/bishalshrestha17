About Me

Get to know more about my journey

I'm a passionate cybersecurity professional and creative digital creator with over 5 years of experience in the tech industry. My journey began with a curiosity about how systems work and how to make them secure, which led me to pursue ethical hacking and cybersecurity.

Over the years, I've expanded my expertise to include graphic design, digital marketing, video editing, and web development. This unique combination of technical security knowledge and creative skills allows me to offer comprehensive solutions to clients.
